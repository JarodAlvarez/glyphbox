GLYPHBOX is a 1-bit fantasy console you can build yourself. Games are written in Lua, printed as Aztec codes on credit-card-sized cards, and read by a camera in the card slot. Play the games or write your own in the browser. The devkit is on GitHub, and the build guide is on its way.
